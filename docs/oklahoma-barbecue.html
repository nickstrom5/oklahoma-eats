From Pauls Valley to the Pearl District

Bob's Pig Shop in Pauls Valley has cooked in its cobblestone cottage since 1933, and Danny's in Ponca City is home to Head Country sauce, created in 1945. Van's Pig Stand began in an old Shawnee service station, and Oakhart Barbecue brought Central Texas-style brisket to Tulsa's Pearl District in 2021.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Oklahoma City (2), Pauls Valley (1), Jenks (1), Wellston (1), Ponca City (1), Bartlesville (1), Hobart (1), Lawton (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a current source backed it up (TravelOK's own pages, the state's tourism site, or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
