Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out on a back road.

What stays on your phone

Oklahoma Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Oklahoma Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Two kinds of source. The onion burger, chicken-fried steak, barbecue, Route 66 and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a current source behind each place: TravelOK's own pages or a dated local story. The wider directory of 9,881 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), kept by the rule that proved reliable in states that publish official lists.

Where's the onion burger?

Our burger list has 15 hand-checked places, from El Reno's fried onion burger counters (Johnnie's Grill, Robert's Grill and Sid's Diner) to Tucker's in Oklahoma City and J&W Grill in Chickasha.

Does it show restaurant inspections?

Yes, as the Oklahoma State Department of Health's PHIN search lists them. Where PHIN lists a place, the app shows its latest inspection's date and how many violations PHIN notes, with PHIN's note that an inspection is a snapshot in time. OSDH gives no score or grade, and the app computes none; no list is ranked by inspections.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

Where a place matches a live on-premises license on the ABLE Commission's monthly lists (mixed beverage, beer and wine, brew pub or hotel beverage), the app says it's licensed to serve on the premises. Otherwise it shows unknown, never no.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
