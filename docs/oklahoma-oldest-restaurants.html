Oklahoma's oldest restaurants

Restaurants and bars founded in 1960 or before and still serving: 6 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, Cattlemen's Steakhouse in Oklahoma City, goes back to 1910.

Which year counts

We count from the year the business opened, as its source gives it. A building's age isn't the business's.
