What counts as an honor here

James Beard Foundation honors, reported as facts rather than scores. The Foundation named Florence's Restaurant in Oklahoma City an America's Classic in 2022, reported as Oklahoma's first James Beard award. Andrew Black of Grey Sweater won Best Chef: Southwest in 2023, and Cat Cox of Country Bird Bakery in Tulsa won Outstanding Pastry Chef or Baker in 2025. In 2026, Jeff Chanchaleune of Bar Sen and Zack Walters of Sedalia's were Best Chef: Southwest finalists, and Later Bye a finalist for Best New Bar. No MICHELIN Guide covers Oklahoma.

James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Oklahoma Eats is not affiliated with it.
