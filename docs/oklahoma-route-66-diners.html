Along the Mother Road

The Rock Cafe in Stroud has served travelers since 1939. POPS in Arcadia stocks more than 700 bottled sodas under a 66-foot pop bottle, Lucille's Roadhouse in Weatherford hand-breads its chicken-fried steak, and Tulsa's Mother Road Market is a food hall on the route.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Tulsa (2), Stroud (1), Sapulpa (1), Elk City (1), Wellston (1), Commerce (1), Weatherford (1), Arcadia (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a current source backed it up (TravelOK's own pages, the state's tourism site, or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.

On your phone, Oklahoma Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
