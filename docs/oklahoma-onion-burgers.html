El Reno's onion burger

El Reno's three counters, Johnnie's Grill, Robert's Grill and Sid's Diner, serve the fried onion burger the town is known for. TravelOK's burger guide also sends readers to Tucker's Onion Burgers in Oklahoma City and J&W Grill in Chickasha for onion burgers, and to Hamburger King in Shawnee, a diner since 1927.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: El Reno (3), Oklahoma City (3), Tulsa (2), Shawnee (1), Sapulpa (1), Lawton (1), Bartlesville (1), Chickasha (1). The dishes we found most often: fried onion burger (3), onion burger (2).

Our method: a place made the list only if a current source backed it up (TravelOK's own pages, the state's tourism site, or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
