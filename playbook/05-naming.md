# Oklahoma Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decisions, 2026-11-07)

## Name
- **Oklahoma Eats** (App Store name "Oklahoma Eats: Restaurants", Nick's choice 2026-11-07, 26 characters; Home Screen "Oklahoma Eats", 13). The prompt's App
  Store check (Sept 2026) found no same-name app.
- Alternatives from the prompt: **Red Dirt Eats** (many firms use "Red Dirt": check TSDR first); **Oklahoma Table**.
- Avoided: "OK Eats" (an "OK EATS" delivery app exists), "Sooner" (reads as OU), "Okie" (a Dust Bowl slur to some), "Imagine That" (the
  plate slogan; TravelOK's #ImagineThatOK) and "OK Here We Go". The Home header's eyebrow is "FROM THE PANHANDLE TO THE RED RIVER".
- Subtitle: "Onion Burgers, BBQ & Route 66" (29). The app's Home tagline is "Onion burgers, BBQ & chicken-fried steak".

## Icon (state-prompts/ICON-STYLE.md; all three pass check.swift; 60 px strip judged)
- **A, Nick's choice (2026-11-07): an El Reno fried onion burger, side view, seared onion strands between patty and bun, on sky blue.** It
  reads at 60 px as a burger, and the onion row makes it Oklahoma's.
- B: a chicken-fried steak under cream gravy, on red dirt. Passes, but at 60 px it reads as a fried egg.
- C: a pecan pie slice from above, on sky blue. Passes, but the pecans read as pepperoni: a pizza slice.
Drawn by `scripts/icon-concepts.swift` (PNGs in `playbook/brand/`); `scripts/make-brand.swift` makes the app icon, site icons and
docs/og.png from A. scripts/make-site.py's LOGO is the same mark as an SVG.

## Palette (the prompt's: OU and OSU split the state, so the flag and the land; no school colours or marks; as built, Nick's yes 2026-11-07)
A deeper sky #00559B (7.6:1 on white) for titles and tint; flag French blue #0072CE for fills and large text only; red dirt #9C3D23
(white on it 6.8:1) for the hand-checked guide cards; ink #1F2A36 for honors chips; shield amber #B9975B only as a fill under ink,
never as text; wheat #F4EBD6 pages. Header band: a row of redbud blossoms (the state tree) in redbud pink with wheat centres on a
sky-blue line; no Osage shield and no tribal designs. Light mode only.
